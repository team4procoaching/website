At 42, Amanda had been told by multiple trainers that her best years for
building muscle were behind her. Irene proved them all wrong.

## Rewriting the Rules

Irene's own career — still competing at the highest level in her fifties — was
living proof that age is not a limitation. She designed a program that respected
Amanda's body while progressively challenging it.

## Patient Progress

Muscle building is a slow game, and Amanda learned to trust the process. Monthly
check-ins showed incremental gains that compounded over time. By month six,
friends and colleagues were noticing visible changes.

## A New Chapter

Twelve months later, Amanda had gained 12 pounds of lean muscle and completely
transformed her physique. More significantly, she had shattered her own beliefs
about what was possible at her age.
