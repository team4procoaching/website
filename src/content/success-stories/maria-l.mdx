Maria had competed once before with a different coach and placed middle of the
pack. She knew her genetics were there — she just needed the right guidance to
bring it all together.

## The Team Advantage

What set Team 4 Pro apart was the collaborative approach. While Helle led her
competition prep, Gina contributed insights on posing, and Irene offered
perspective on muscle maturity. Three elite minds working toward one goal.

## Dialing In the Details

Over 20 weeks, every aspect of Maria's physique was refined. Training splits
were adjusted weekly based on progress photos and measurements. Nutrition was
periodized to maximize muscle retention while achieving stage-ready
conditioning.

## Competition Day

Maria placed top three in her figure class — a dramatic improvement from her
previous attempt. The difference was not just physical conditioning, but the
confidence that comes from knowing three champions believe in your potential.
