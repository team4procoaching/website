Rachel came to Team 4 Pro not with a specific number in mind, but with a feeling
she wanted to leave behind — the constant fatigue, the stress eating, the sense
that her health was slowly slipping away.

## A Holistic Starting Point

Gina did not start with a meal plan. She started with a conversation about
Rachel's daily life — her stress triggers, her sleep patterns, her relationship
with food. The program was built around Rachel's reality, not an idealized
version of it.

## Building New Habits

The first three months focused on fundamentals: consistent sleep, regular
movement, and balanced nutrition. No extreme restrictions. Rachel was surprised
by how much changed simply by getting the basics right.

## The Ripple Effect

By month nine, the transformation was unmistakable — not just in the mirror, but
in every aspect of Rachel's life. Better energy, improved mood, stronger
relationships. The physical changes were almost a side effect of a fundamentally
healthier way of living.
