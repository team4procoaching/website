At 52, Dana had accepted that her best physical years were behind her. A
friend's recommendation led her to Irene — and that single conversation changed
everything.

## Training With Wisdom

Irene's approach was unlike anything Dana had experienced. Every exercise
selection considered joint health, recovery capacity, and hormonal changes.
Training was intense but intelligent, pushing boundaries without breaking them.

## Nutrition for the Long Game

Rather than aggressive calorie deficits, Irene designed a nutrition protocol
that supported muscle growth while gradually reducing body fat. The emphasis was
on nutrient density, timing, and consistency — not deprivation.

## Proving What Is Possible

Ten months later, Dana had added 8 pounds of muscle and dropped 15 pounds of
fat. At 52, she was genuinely in the best shape of her life — stronger, leaner,
and more confident than she had been in decades.
